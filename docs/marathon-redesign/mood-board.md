# Mood board — thock × MARATHON

## Thesis

The MARATHON look is **flat, institutional, tracked, acid, and deadpan**. Flat: hard rectangles, zero radius, no shadow or gradient — depth comes from a four-step charcoal ladder, not from light. Institutional: every label reads as though a bureaucracy issued it — batch numbers, index prefixes, counts — so the chrome is a *record*, not a decoration. Tracked: uppercase monospace with letter-spacing is the connective tissue, doing the work borders and icons do in ordinary SaaS. Acid: one fluorescent hue carries all emotional weight against near-black, and it is scarce — when acid appears, something is selected, primary, or dirty. Deadpan: the joke ("MINIMAL FLAVOUR / MAXIMUM FUNCTION") is played straight. What it is **not**: not cyberpunk-neon (no glow, bloom, scanline haze), not retro-pixel-cute (the 8-bit glyphs are marks, not a theme), not gradient-glass or rounded-friendly, not maximalist per screen (the posters are maximal; the chrome is severe), and not illegible-brutalist. thock is a settings tool. The posters are the voice; the VAULT screen is the law.

The acid token is **`#c2fd0a`**, ΔE2000 0.41 from the poster lime `#c0fe04` — visually the same colour,
numerically not. Four delivered SVGs bake the decorative `#c0fe04`; see thock-style-plan §5.

**Two hard rules from 09.** Colour is never the only cue: selection keeps its black `[1]`, dirty carries `● UNSAVED`, errors the `ERR:` prefix, tiers `STD`/`RARE`/`EPIC`. Cobalt-vs-purple is the set's worst collision (protan ΔE 4.4, deutan 4.5 — visually identical), with orange/red colliding under deutan and red/magenta under tritan, so those abbreviations are mandatory. Focus is `outline: 2px solid acid; outline-offset: 2px` plus a **2px** void moat (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--background)` — it has to equal the offset to fill the gap; 09 wrote 1px against a 1px offset), so the ring self-contrasts on any light fill.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

1. Make acid scarce. Solid acid fill is spent on exactly four things: selected keys/tiles on the stage, the Apply button, the landing `CONNECT` block, the active profile chip. Active tabs and nav rows get a 2px acid bar instead; the dirty card bar gets 3px. Two acid fills competing on one screen is a bug.
2. Pin chrome to the four edges and leave the stage empty.
3. Set every chrome label in 11px tracked uppercase mono with tabular numerals.
4. Prefix every nav item and section heading with `[n]`, and right-align a count.
5. Show keyboard hints as square `<kbd>` chips beside the thing they operate.
6. Build hierarchy from the charcoal ladder and type weight — nothing else.
7. Pair every hover background change with a border appearing.
8. Write errors as `ERR: <WHAT WENT WRONG>` in caps.
9. Keep the fictional voice in captions only, never in a control's own label.
10. Gate decorative motion behind `motion-safe:`, cap it at 200ms, never loop it.

**Don't**

1. Don't use the posters' bright fields as a page background — accent blocks only.
2. Don't add glow, bloom, scanlines or shadow. Ever.
3. Don't use a pixel font for real UI text.
4. Don't let Anton near a control label or a value.
5. Don't put fake batch numbers, rulers or swatch strips near a real number — a decorative `N3` beside a live DPI reading is a usability bug.
6. Don't encode tier or state in colour alone.
7. Don't use orange, red or magenta *text* below 14px semibold — APCA caps them near Lc47.
8. Don't let broken grids or overlapping type out of the landing page.
9. Don't add notches, chamfers or angular clips (augmented-ui style) — MARATHON's panels are plain rectangles. The only corner marks are the registration ticks on the stage frame and ConnectGate panel, in muted grey.
10. Don't chase per-screen novelty. Fourteen settings pages must read as one document.

## Provenance and caveats

- **No Bungie brand guide exists in our sources.** Every MARATHON hex was scraped or derived by third-party tooling. The lime `#C0FE04` is corroborated across three independent sources and is the safest value we have — *derived*, not official.
- **The poster hexes are unverified** — eyeball estimates from key art; no published list specific to the posters exists. They are also unusable as shipped: discovery/09 measured white-on-`#ff5a1f` at 2.68:1 and white-on-`#e5259a` at 4.15:1, both failing AA. Our palette is contrast-derived, not poster-matched.
- **Locked-vs-measured acid — resolved.** D2 specifies `oklch(92% .23 125)`; discovery/09's figures were measured at `oklch(92% .22 128)` (`#baff3f`). Re-run at the locked value: it is **inside sRGB** and resolves to **`#c2fd0a`** — 16.46:1 / Lc‑94.0 on void, 17.35:1 / Lc 93.5 for black on acid. Every 09 verdict holds; the deltas are under 1 Lc. The palette board and styling-plan §5 carry the recomputed numbers.
- **Art-director quotes versus press framing.** "Graphic Realism" and the influence list (tDR, Otomo, Morimoto, Cunningham, Wipeout, Ghost in the Shell) are attributable to Joseph Cross via 80.lv. "Wipeout meets Ghost in the Shell" is a headline, not his phrasing. **"Swiss design", "Y2K", "brutalism" and "Metahaven" appear in no Bungie source** — only third-party criticism. The lineage above is our argument, not Bungie's.
- **The antire.al controversy.** Bungie acknowledged a former artist included work by Fern "Antireal" Hook in a texture sheet used in-game; one outlet reports the dispute resolved. Consequence: study the poster *grammar* — grid, dither, mono captions, one hero colour — and originate our own artwork. Never trace or re-colour a specific poster.
- **Inferred UI details.** discovery/10 flags slider, toggle, dropdown and dialog geometry as inferred by extension, not observed; where our plans specify those, it is a consistent invention, not a reproduction. And Cross left Bungie roughly three months before the March 2026 launch, so the shipped game is not wholly the identity he described — our reference is the alpha/marketing-era look these four images capture.
